Common questions

How do I pair with my partner?

One of you creates an invite code in the app and shares it. The other enters the code. Each person can have one active pairing at a time.

My steps are not showing up.

Make sure StepDare has permission to read Steps in the Health app (Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > StepDare). Open the app once so it can sync. If your partner's steps are late, ask them to open StepDare too.

How do I unpair or delete my data?

You can unpair in the Profile tab; this permanently deletes your shared game history. To delete your account and all associated data, email us from the address linked to your account and we will remove it.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone. Premium is shared between both partners in a couple.
