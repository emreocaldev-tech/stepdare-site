Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

StepDare ("the app") is a daily game for couples, powered by your step counts. It is developed and operated by StepDare ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: stepdare.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple or email, we receive your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address) and an account identifier.
  • Profile. The display name, avatar icon and usage goal you choose during setup.
  • Daily step totals. With your permission, StepDare reads your total step count for each day from Apple Health (HealthKit) and from your device's motion sensors for the live counter. We store only the daily total number. We do not store or upload raw health data, heart rate, workouts, location or any other Health data type.
  • Game data. Your pairing with your partner, the daily dare you play, daily results, streaks, badges, and nudges you send each other.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, a device token so we can send game updates (for example, your partner's progress and day results).
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe to StepDare Premium, our subscription provider processes your purchase information (such as your anonymous profile ID and purchase receipt) to tell us whether Premium is active. We never see your payment card details; payments are handled by Apple.

How we use it

  • To run the game: compare steps with your partner, pick the winner, track streaks, and show your widget.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To provide and verify your Premium subscription.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to track you across other apps or websites. Health-related data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared with data brokers.

What your partner can see

When you pair with someone, your partner can see your display name, avatar, your daily step total, game results, and streaks. They cannot see any other Health data.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and authentication (servers in the United States).
  • Adapty processes subscription purchases and Premium status.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, push notifications (APNs) and payments.

These providers process data only to deliver their service to us. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Retention and deletion

  • Unpairing. If you leave a pairing from the Profile tab, the shared game history of that pair (dares, results, badges, nudges) is permanently deleted.
  • Account deletion. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your profile, your daily step totals, your push notification tokens and the shared game history with your partner (dares, results, streaks, badges, nudges and Premium status for that pair). Your partner stays on StepDare unpaired and keeps their own account. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage or cancel it in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. If you cannot use the app, email stepdare.app@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • You can revoke Health access at any time in the Health app (Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices) and turn off notifications in iOS Settings.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access rules that limit each user to their own and their partner's data. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

StepDare is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Email us and we will respond.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
